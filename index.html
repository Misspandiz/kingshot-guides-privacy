Privacy Policy for Kingshot Guides

Effective Date: November 5, 2025

Last Updated: November 5, 2025

App Version: 2.1.1

Kingshot Guides (“we”, “our”, “us”) provides tools and guides for players of the Kingshot game. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, the third parties we rely on, and your rights.

1) Information We Collect

  • Player ID and Nickname: Used to personalize in-app calculators, guides, and saved preferences. Stored locally and may sync securely via Firebase.
  • Email (Google Sign-In only): Used for authentication and account recovery; handled by Google Sign-In. We do not see your password.
  • Feedback via Discord Webhook: If you submit feedback, your message and provided nickname are sent over HTTPS to our Discord server.
  • Analytics & Crash Reports: Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collect pseudonymous usage data and crash diagnostics to improve stability and features.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob may use device identifiers (AAID/IDFA) for contextual or (with consent where required) personalized ads.
  • Device & App Info: Basic technical data such as app version, device model, OS version, language, and country (non-precise) for performance and localization.

2) How We Use Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve app functionality
  • Personalize guides, calculators, and saved preferences
  • Respond to support requests and feedback
  • Monitor performance, fix bugs, and prevent abuse
  • Display, measure, and cap frequency of advertising
  • Operate optional premium features and purchase entitlements

3) Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your data. We share information with service providers only to operate the app:

  • Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics)
  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
  • Google Sign-In (Authentication)
  • Discord (Feedback Webhooks)

Each third party processes data under its own privacy policy and applicable terms.

4) Persistent Identifiers

We may use persistent identifiers such as Firebase Installation IDs and Ad IDs for analytics, debugging, fraud prevention, and advertising. These IDs do not contain your name, email, or contact details.

5) Premium Features & Purchases

Some optional features (e.g., supporter role, ad-free experience, or early access) may be available through in-app purchases or donations handled by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment card details.

  • Entitlements: We store purchase status/entitlements (e.g., premium unlocked) to enable features on your devices.
  • Subscriptions (if applicable): If we offer subscriptions, the price, billing interval, trial length (if any), renewal, cancellation, and any benefits are clearly disclosed by Google Play before purchase.
  • Refunds/Cancellation: Managed through your Google Play account settings subject to Google Play policies.

6) Age & Child Safety

Kingshot Guides is intended for a general audience and does not include age-restricted content such as dating or real-money gambling.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the age required by your local law). If you believe a child provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7) Legal Bases (EEA/UK only)

  • Performance of a contract: To provide the app and premium features you request.
  • Legitimate interests: App security, fraud prevention, analytics, debugging, and service improvements.
  • Consent: Personalized advertising where required by law (you can withdraw consent at any time within app or device settings).

8) International Transfers

Our processors may store data in countries outside your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses) offered by our service providers.

9) Security

All data transmission uses HTTPS. Authentication via Google Sign-In uses OAuth 2.0. Discord webhook data is restricted to designated channels. While no method is 100% secure, we implement reasonable measures to protect your information.

10) Data Retention

  • Player preferences and entitlements: retained while you use the app and for a reasonable period thereafter for re-installs and fraud prevention.
  • Analytics and crash logs: retained by Firebase per their standard retention windows.
  • Feedback messages: retained for support history and abuse prevention, then deleted or anonymized.

11) Your Choices & Controls

  • Clear saved Player ID and cache within the app.
  • Disable personalized ads in device settings (Reset/Limit Ad ID) and/or via consent prompts where applicable.
  • Revoke Google Sign-In access from your Google account settings.
  • Opt out of analytics where supported by your device or OS settings.

12) Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, and to data portability. To exercise rights, contact us at the email below. We may take steps to verify your request.

13) Developer & Store Declarations

We complete the required Google Play declarations (including the Declaration on Financial Functions) for this app. This relates to our Play Console compliance and does not grant us access to your payment card details.

14) Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post updates on this page and adjust the “Last Updated” date above. Material changes will be highlighted in-app or via an appropriate notice.
